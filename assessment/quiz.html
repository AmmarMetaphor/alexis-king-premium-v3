The 3 Ps of Performance Assessment

How effectively are you prioritizing, practicing, and persisting?

Rate each statement based on how accurately it reflects your current behavior.

1 Rarely2 Occasionally3 Sometimes4 Often5 Consistently

Answer honestly based on what you actually do, not what you intend to do.

Step 1 of 3Prioritize

1. I can identify the tasks and responsibilities that will have the greatest impact on my goals.

2. I make clear decisions about what requires my attention now and what can wait.

3. I protect time for important work instead of allowing urgent requests to control my schedule.

4. I regularly reassess my priorities when expectations, goals, or circumstances change.

5. I am comfortable saying no, delegating, or renegotiating work that does not align with my most important objectives.

Please answer all five questions before continuing.

Step 2 of 3Practice

6. I intentionally work on the skills I need to remain effective and relevant.

7. I am willing to try unfamiliar approaches, tools, or technologies before I feel completely confident.

8. I seek feedback that can help me improve my performance.

9. I create regular opportunities to apply what I am learning instead of relying only on information or training.

10. I view mistakes as useful information that can help me adjust and improve.

Please answer all five questions before continuing.

Step 3 of 3Persistence

11. I continue taking meaningful action even when progress is slower than expected.

12. I recover and refocus after setbacks, disappointments, or unexpected changes.

13. I can adjust my approach without abandoning the goal.

14. I maintain healthy boundaries and manage my energy so I can sustain my performance over time.

15. I remain committed to my growth even when I do not receive immediate recognition or results.

Please answer all five questions before continuing.

Keynote 01

Thriving Through Change

Stay effective when the structure shifts but the expectations do not.

A reorganization is announced. Roles move. Priorities compete. The deadlines are still there.

That is when capable people can lose momentum because everything suddenly feels urgent and nothing feels certain.

Thriving Through Change gives people a practical way to decide what matters now, communicate their value as roles evolve, and keep delivering while the organization finds its footing.

Alexis uses The Career Ninja Mindset® and the 3 Ps to move the audience from reacting to change toward making deliberate decisions inside it.

  • A practical way to separate what matters most from what simply feels urgent.
  • A clearer approach for communicating their contribution when roles, expectations, or priorities are changing.
  • The 3 Ps framework for making progress even before every answer is available.
  • Pause before reacting and identify the decision that actually needs to be made.
  • Protect attention for the work that creates the most value instead of allowing every new request to become the priority.
  • Keep moving with an adjustable plan rather than waiting for certainty.

Organizations navigating:

  • restructuring or reorganization
  • leadership or role changes
  • shifting business priorities
  • rapid growth
  • post change fatigue
  • teams being asked to maintain performance during uncertainty

Keynote 02

Thriving Through AI Disruption

Turn uncertainty about AI into a plan for staying useful, visible, and ready.

AI may be on the organization's roadmap, but for employees the question is often much more personal: what happens to my role?

Thriving Through AI Disruption moves the conversation away from panic and toward practical decisions.

Alexis helps audiences identify what they need to learn, where technology can remove low value work, and which human capabilities become more important as the tools get better.

The goal is not to predict every change AI will create. It is to help people keep building the judgment, skills, visibility, and value that make them difficult to overlook.

  • A clearer way to identify which capabilities deserve their learning time now.
  • A practical lens for deciding what technology can handle and where human judgment still creates value.
  • A plan for staying visible and relevant as roles and workflows change.
  • Replace passive worry about AI with one specific skill building action.
  • Start experimenting with useful tools before confidence is complete.
  • Make contribution and results more visible instead of assuming good work will speak for itself.

Organizations and audiences experiencing:

  • AI adoption
  • automation
  • digital transformation
  • changing job expectations
  • workforce reskilling
  • concern about professional relevance

Keynote 03

From Invisible to Invincible

Make strong work visible without becoming someone you are not.

Some of the most capable people in an organization are also the easiest to overlook.

They prepare more than they speak. They wait until the idea is perfect. They take on extra work but hesitate to name the value they create.

Over time, quiet competence can become professional invisibility.

From Invisible to Invincible helps people break that pattern without turning self advocacy into self promotion.

Alexis shows audiences how to make their contribution easier to see, speak about their value with clarity, and practice visibility before the stakes are high.

The goal is not to become louder. It is to stop making valuable work difficult to notice.

  • A practical way to make contributions and results more visible.
  • Language for communicating value without over explaining or overselling.
  • A repeatable approach for practicing confidence before high stakes moments.
  • Stop waiting until an idea feels perfect before contributing.
  • Replace invisible effort with clearer communication about outcomes and impact.
  • Practice asking, speaking, presenting, and advocating in smaller moments before the opportunity becomes critical.
  • emerging leaders
  • high performers who struggle with visibility
  • employee resource groups
  • leadership development programs
  • professionals preparing for greater responsibility
  • teams focused on confidence, communication, and career growth

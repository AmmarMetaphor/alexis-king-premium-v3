What she believes

Motivation is not a decision system.

Change rarely arrives at a convenient time. When it does, fear can look like procrastination, perfectionism, or playing small.

I do not believe the answer is more motivation. People need a practical way to decide what matters, build the skills the moment requires, and keep moving when the path changes.

That is the thinking behind Prioritize, Practice, and Persistence: focus the work, strengthen the capability, and keep going without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.
